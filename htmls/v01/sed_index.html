Blackbody SED cross-calibration

Nine versions of the multi-band blackbody SED fit on the COSMOS clean star sample. Each version differs in which bands constrain the fit and which are predicted-and-tested. Click a version name to open its paginated SED-card webform; click "summary plot" to open just the per-band offset figure.

Sample for all versions: 6,007 clean stars from the 4-way HST∩JWST∩Euclid intersection with SNR > 10 in all 9 HST/JWST/Euclid bands. In v03 onward the 274 AGN/QSO are excluded from the calibration statistics and the main star webform, but their photometry is kept in a separate index_agn.html page. AB magnitudes throughout; blackbody fit by 1-D χ2 scan in T with the flux normalisation solved per-T.

v01 fit: HST + JWST (5 bands) test: Euclid VIS/Y/J/H [summary plot]

First-order Euclid offset test. Fit a blackbody to HST F814W + JWST F115/F150/F277/F444W only, then predict the four Euclid bands and measure Δ. Result: VIS +0.16, Y −0.09, J −0.13, H −0.18 mag.

Anchor-only test — no other facilities yet, no AGN cut.

v02 fit: COSMOS-Web HST + JWST (5) test: CFHT u, HSC, UltraVISTA, IRAC, MIRI (15) [summary plot]

Multi-telescope extension using COSMOS-Web's own HST F814W + JWST flux_model as anchor. Predict every other facility recorded in the COSMOS-Web mastercatalog and measure Δ per band. Validity filter rejects flux_err sentinels and per-source fill values.

Anchor differs from our Step-3 photometry; the optical "blue trend" here is partly an anchor effect (see v03).

v03 fit: OUR HST + JWST (5) test: Euclid + CFHT + HSC + IRAC + MIRI (15) [summary plot] [AGN page]

Same as v01 but with the multi-facility test set added (our Euclid + COSMOS-Web CFHT/HSC/IRAC/MIRI). Anchored on our Step-3 HST F814W + JWST NIRCam. AGN/QSO excluded from offsets.

Optical HSC offsets snap to ≈0 vs v02 → our HST+JWST photometry differs slightly from COSMOS-Web's anchor.

v04 fit: ALL 20 bands (democratic, no anchor) [summary plot] [AGN page]

Global blackbody fit to every available band per star (HST + JWST + Euclid + CFHT + HSC + IRAC + MIRI = 20). No band privileged. Per-band Δ shows each facility's deviation from the all-facility consensus.

Bottom line: almost all bands within ±0.15 mag of the global continuum; the coherent ≈±0.15 mag wavelength ripple is the blackbody-vs-real-star shape, not calibration error.

v05 fit: v04 bands minus Euclid (16) held out: Euclid VIS / Y / J / H [summary plot] [AGN page]

Like v04 but the four Euclid bands are held out of the fit. Blackbody anchored on every other facility, then Euclid is predicted from the consensus continuum and tested.

v06 fit: v04 + SDSS u/g/r/i/z (25) [summary plot] [AGN page]

v04 extended with SDSS DR16 PSF magnitudes. Of the 6,007 stars, 588–1,098 have SDSS detections per band (depth-limited). SDSS bands are fit democratically with the others.

New constraints: SDSS u −0.17, g −0.09, r 0.00, i −0.04, z −0.24. The SDSS z offset is consistent with the known PS1/SDSS z step vs HSC.

v07 fit: v04 + PS1 g/r/i/z/y (25) [summary plot] [AGN page]

v04 extended with Pan-STARRS DR1 mean PSF magnitudes (VizieR II/349). 398–1,053 PS1 detections per band.

PS1 r −0.07, i −0.13, z −0.28, y −0.52. PS1 y is the standout (wide PSF, partial COSMOS coverage, well-known soft calibration vs other surveys).

v08 fit: v04 + SDSS + PS1 — ALL 30 BANDS [summary plot] [AGN page]

All ten facilities at once: HST + JWST + Euclid + CFHT + HSC + IRAC + MIRI + SDSS + PS1. The fullest democratic blackbody fit in the series.

Optical SDSS/PS1 g-band cluster around −0.1; HSC/Euclid sit slightly above zero; IRAC ch1/ch2 and MIRI agree to <0.15 mag — cross-facility consistency at the ≈0.15 mag level is preserved even with 30 bands in the fit.

The strongest Euclid test in the series: blackbody anchored on twenty-six bands from every other facility (HST + JWST + CFHT + HSC + IRAC + MIRI + SDSS + PS1), then Euclid predicted and tested.
